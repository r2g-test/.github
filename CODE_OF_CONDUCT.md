# Code of conduct

We are committed to a collaborative environment where people can contribute without harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries.
- Make space for different experience levels and communication styles.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully and use documented escalation paths when disagreement remains.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, deliberate sabotage, or retaliation against good-faith reporters is not acceptable in organization spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to organization maintainers or owners. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, revoke access, or refer serious matters to the relevant platform or authorities.

Enforcement decisions should consider severity, pattern, impact, safety, and the need to protect reporters and participants. Retaliation for a good-faith report is itself a violation.
